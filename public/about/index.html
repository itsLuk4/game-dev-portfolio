About

About Me

Hi! I’m Luka, a passionate Game Developer & Designer. I am new to the industry and have a burning passion to start my career.

🧙‍♂️ My Journey

I am a Computer Engineering graduate transitioning into game development. I am driven by a love for story‑driven RPGs, I always dreamt of learning and creating games. My passion for games was always strong, but my path was a bit curvy. With a lot of soul searching and past job hopping I realized my dream still burns bright, reminding me that my calling is still in the gaming industry.

👨‍💻 What I Do

At the moment I am learning everything:

  • Gameplay Programming — Building responsive, fun-to-play core mechanics
  • Systems Design — Architecting game systems that scale and interact elegantly
  • Level Design — Crafting engaging player spaces and pacing
  • Technical Design — Bridging the gap between design vision and technical implementation
  • Prototyping — Rapid iteration to find the fun fast
  • Testing - Exploitations, bugs and how fun the features feel etc.

📖 Philosophy

From my all time favourite quotes in Mass Effect:

“Nobody ever fell in love without being a little brave.” — Commander Shepard

“I won’t let fear compromise who I am.” - Commander Shepard

I believe great games emerge from the intersection of team collaboration, communication, relentless playtesting and having fun while doing it. I’m drawn to projects where gameplay and narrative reinforce each other, and where player agency creates meaningful stories.
