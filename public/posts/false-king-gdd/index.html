False King 2D — Game Design Document

Status: Concept Complete — Ready for prototyping

Game Design Concept

Working Title

False King 2D

Concept Statement

An arcade platformer where you jump, swing a hammer, collect diamonds, and survive traps to reclaim your cursed castle.

Genre

Arcade / Platform

Target Audience

Players who enjoy short arcade sessions. Any age and gender.

Unique Selling Points

Feature Description
Hammer Combat Swing hammer to defeat pig enemies
Diamond Pickups Collect diamonds for score
Traps Spikes, pits, moving hazards
Enemy Variety Different pig behaviors

Player Experience & Game POV

  • Role: The False King — rightful heir cursed by Lord Pigsturr
  • Story: Your ascension interrupted; castle inhabitants turned into aggressive pigs. Reclaim the castle, lift the curse.
  • Vibe: Arcade platforming — precise jumps, satisfying hammer hits, diamond chasing
  • Perspective: Side-scrolling 2D

Platform

Portfolio piece on GitHub Pages (WebGL), playable on PC and web.

Objectives

Traverse levels door-to-door, defeat pig enemies, collect diamonds, reach the finish line.

Core Loops

  1. Enter Level → 2. Jump & Dash → 2. Hammer Enemies → 3. Collect Diamonds → 3. Avoid Traps → 4. Reach Door → 4. Score & Next Level

Engagement from tight platforming, satisfying hammer hits, and score chasing.

Interactivity

  • Enemies (pigs) patrol, charge, or throw projectiles
  • Traps: spikes, pits, moving blades, crumbling platforms
  • Diamonds scattered; some hidden in risky spots
  • Doors act as level finish lines

Platform & Deployment

  • Primary: GitHub Pages (WebGL build via Unity)
  • Scope: Portfolio showcase — free, no monetization
