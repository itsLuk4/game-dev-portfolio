False King 2D

Gameplay Programmer & Designer

game portfolio platform arcade 2d

Overview

False King 2D is an arcade platformer where you play as the rightful heir — cursed by Lord Pigsturr, your arch‑nemesis, who turned the entire castle into mindless, aggressive pigs. Jump, dash, swing your hammer, collect diamonds, and survive traps to reclaim your castle and lift the curse.

My Role

As Gameplay Programmer & Designer, I:

  • Built tight 2D platforming movement (jump, dash)
  • Designed hammer combat with hit‑pause and knock‑back feel
  • Created diamond scoring & risk‑reward placement

Key Features

  • Hammer Combat — satisfying swing with hit‑pause, knock‑back, and pig‑squish feedback
  • Diamond Pickups — score currency; some hidden in risky spots
  • Traps — Bombs
  • Enemy Variety — walkers
  • Level Flow — door‑to‑door runs, each ending at a finish door

Core Loops

  1. Enter Level → 2. Jump & Dash → 3. Hammer Enemies → 4. Collect Diamonds → 5. Avoid Traps → 6. Reach Door → Score & Next Level

Engagement from tight platforming, satisfying combat, and score chasing.

Tech Stack

  • Language: C#
  • CI/CD: GitHub Actions → GitHub Pages deploy
